ES International exists to empower young people with the skills, mindset, and opportunities to lead and create lasting change

Who Are We?

ES International is a youth-centered non-profit organization focused on developing ethical, innovative, and impact-driven leaders across Africa through leadership development, mentorship, creativity, and entrepreneurship.

Why We Exist

ES International was born from a desire to bridge the leadership and innovation gap among Africa's youth. We recognize that the future of our continent lies in equipping its youngest citizens with the tools, mindset, and opportunities to thrive and lead effectively in all spheres of life; education, business, politics, arts, and civil society.

Aims & Objectives

Explore Potential

To help young people explore, discover, and enhance their potential through debates, drama, public speaking, and performing arts

Creative Spirit

To instill a creative and entrepreneurial spirit in teenagers and youth.

Nurture Leaders

To nurture the next generation of transformational leaders across Africa.

Humanitarian Support

To extend humanitarian support and advocacy to promote equity, inclusion, and human dignity.
